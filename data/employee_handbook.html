Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Introduction

Acme Corp is a remote-first company. We believe that you can do your best work from anywhere. We value clear communication, long-term thinking, and treating everyone with respect.

Benefits & Perks

Time Off

We believe in a healthy work-life balance. * Vacation: You get 3 weeks of paid vacation per year. * Holidays: We observe all major national holidays. * Sabbatical: Every 3 years, you are eligible for a 30-day paid sabbatical.

Medical Leave (FMLA)

Acme Corp complies with the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). * Eligibility: Employees who have worked for at least 12 months and 1,250 hours. * Entitlement: Up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for: * Birth and care of a newborn child of the employee. * Placement with the employee of a child for adoption or foster care. * Care for an immediate family member (spouse, child, or parent) with a serious health condition. * Medical leave when the employee is unable to work because of a serious health condition (e.g., surgery, recovery). * Short-Term Disability: We offer short-term disability insurance that pays 60% of your salary for up to 12 weeks while you are on medical leave. * How to Apply: Contact HR (hr@acmecorp.com) at least 30 days in advance when foreseeable.

Wellness

  • Health Insurance: We cover 100% of premiums for you and your family.
  • Fitness Allowance: $100/month for gym memberships or fitness equipment.

Policies

Moonlighting & Conflict of Interest

We want you to be versatile, but we must protect the company's integrity. * Moonlighting: You are allowed to work on side projects or freelance work under the following conditions: 1. It does not compete with Acme Corp. 2. It does not interfere with your primary duties here. 3. You do not use Acme Corp resources or equipment for your side work. * Conflict of Interest: You must disclose any potential conflicts. This includes: * Ownership in a competitor or vendor. * Serving on the board of a competitor. * Hiring or supervising a family member (see Nepotism). * Reporting: If you are unsure, submit a Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form to your manager or HR for review.

Hiring Relatives (Nepotism)

Acme Corp permits the hiring of relatives, provided: 1. No Direct Reporting: Relatives cannot report directly to one another. 2. Recusal: Employees must recuse themselves from hiring decisions involving their relatives. 3. Disclosure: The relationship must be disclosed to HR prior to an offer being made. Definition of Relative: Spouse, child, parent, sibling, domestic partner.

Remote Work & International Travel

Acme Corp is a remote company. * Location: You can work from anywhere with a reliable internet connection. * International Work: You may work from an international location for up to 30 days per year without special tax processing. * Over 30 days: Requires prior approval from HR and Finance due to potential tax implications for both you and the company (Nexus laws). * Data Security: You must use the company VPN at all times. * Approvals: Inform your manager at least 2 weeks before international travel.

Payroll & Compensation

  • Pay Schedule: Bi-weekly, every other Friday.
  • Direct Deposit: Managed via the ADP Portal. Changes take 1 pay cycle to process.
  • Pay Discrepancies: If your paycheck seems incorrect:
    1. Check your pay stub in ADP for tax code changes or benefit deductions.
    2. If it remains unresolved, submit a ticket to Payroll (payroll@acmecorp.com).
    3. Notify your manager if it impacts your livelihood immediately.

Equipment

We provide every employee with a new laptop (MacBook Pro or similar) and a display. You can replace your machine every 3 years. You own the equipment after 3 years of service.

Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment. * Harassment: We have a zero-tolerance policy for harassment of any kind. * Communication: Be kind, strictly avoid personal attacks, and assume positive intent.
